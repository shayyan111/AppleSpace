Who we are

This policy applies to the AppleSpace website and the customer information connected to it. Contact us on WhatsApp at +92 334 5136382, or visit Shop 103 & 104, Mezzanine Floor, Noor Mobile Mall, 6th Road, Rawalpindi.

02

What we collect

Order requests include your name, phone number, selected products and quantities, collection or delivery preference, delivery address if needed, and any notes you provide. Review submissions include your display name, phone number, product or service, rating, review, publication permission and optional WhatsApp preference. We record submission and publication dates and consent timestamps. Staff sign-in information is used for the restricted website portal.

The shopping bag is saved in your browser’s local storage. Temporary submission references and staff sessions use session storage. Hosting and service providers may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs to operate and protect the website. The website currently does not collect card details or take online payments.

03

Why we use it

We use information to respond to enquiries, process and follow up on order requests, arrange agreed collection or delivery, provide support, moderate reviews, maintain customer records, and prevent misuse or duplicate submissions.

If you select the optional WhatsApp checkbox, your name and phone number are added to our customer messaging list, or matched to an existing customer. We record your opt-in to offers, updates and greetings. Leaving it unchecked does not enroll a new contact. It does not cancel an earlier opt-in; message us to withdraw that permission.

04

Reviews and sharing

Only approved reviews are public. Your display name, product or service, rating, review and publication date may appear online with your publication permission. Your phone number and messaging preference are not shown publicly. Please keep private details out of the review text.

Authorized staff use the shared store database to manage orders and customer records. Database and hosting providers process information needed to run the service. A delivery provider receives the contact and address details needed for an agreed delivery. If you use WhatsApp, Instagram or another external link, that service’s own privacy terms also apply.

05

Retention, choices and security

We retain information while needed for customer support, transaction records, dispute handling and applicable legal or accounting obligations. No fixed automatic deletion period is currently promised. You can ask us to review, correct or delete your information, remove a published review, or stop WhatsApp marketing. Some transaction records may need to be retained.

To make a request, contact us on WhatsApp and identify the relevant order or review. We may ask for enough information to verify that the record belongs to you. We restrict staff access and use secure connections, but no online system can guarantee absolute security. You can clear browser storage to remove your locally saved bag; this does not delete records already submitted to us.
