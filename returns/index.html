01

Before you purchase

Confirm the exact unit’s condition, storage, battery health, PTA and carrier-lock status, included items, and checking warranty before completing your purchase. The applicable warranty period, return eligibility, any change-of-mind allowance and any agreed costs must be confirmed in writing with your invoice or order confirmation before payment. This website does not advertise a single return window for every product.

Keep your invoice and the written terms for your purchase. A checking warranty is not automatically the same as a refund entitlement or an Apple manufacturer warranty.

02

Report a problem

If an item arrives damaged, is incorrect, differs from the agreed description, or develops a fault, contact us as soon as you notice the issue. Send your invoice or order reference, a description of the problem, and helpful photos or video on WhatsApp. Do not post your phone’s IMEI or other private information in a public review.

Our team will explain the next steps and any inspection needed. Contact us before sending an item back so we can confirm the return address, handling instructions and who will pay any courier charges. Original packaging and included items help us check what was supplied; their absence does not automatically remove rights provided by applicable law.

03

Assessment and outcome

Requests are assessed against the product’s agreed description, the written terms supplied at purchase and applicable consumer rights. Depending on the issue, an appropriate outcome may be a repair, replacement, refund or another agreed resolution. We will explain the decision and, where a refund is agreed, its amount, method and expected processing time.

Change-of-mind returns are subject to the allowance agreed before purchase. Damage caused after purchase, liquid exposure, misuse, unauthorized modifications or disclosed limitations may affect a checking-warranty claim. These factors do not create a blanket exclusion of rights available under applicable law.

04

Cancellation and your rights

Website checkout currently sends an order request without taking payment or reserving stock. Contact us if you want to cancel or change that request. For an order already confirmed or dispatched, our team will explain the available options and any charges previously agreed.

Nothing in this policy limits consumer rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded. Later policy updates do not replace the written terms already agreed for your purchase.
