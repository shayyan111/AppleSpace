Order requests and confirmation

Checkout submits a request to AppleSpace. It does not take online payment, reserve stock or guarantee dispatch. Our team confirms availability, the product details, final total, payment arrangement and collection or delivery with you before the order is finalized. Prices are shown in Pakistani rupees (PKR).

02

Collect from the shop

Select collection at checkout and contact us to arrange your visit. Our shop is at Shop 103 & 104, Mezzanine Floor, Noor Mobile Mall, 6th Road, Rawalpindi. Confirm opening hours and availability on WhatsApp before travelling. You can discuss the unit’s condition, included items and checking warranty with our team.

03

Delivery availability, charges and timing

Delivery availability depends on your location and the item. We confirm the service area, delivery charge, courier arrangement and estimated dispatch and arrival times before you commit to the order. Delivery charges are not included in the website’s product subtotal unless expressly confirmed. We do not currently publish a nationwide flat rate or guaranteed delivery schedule.

Provide an accurate address and reachable phone number. If your details change, tell us before dispatch. Courier delays, weather and other disruptions can affect estimates. Contact us for a status update; tracking details can be shared where provided by the courier.

04

Receiving your order

Check the package and supplied items when they arrive. Report visible damage, missing items or an incorrect unit promptly with your order reference and photos. Packaging photos or an unboxing video may help us investigate but are not a blanket condition for exercising applicable consumer rights. See our Return & Refund Policy for the request process.

If a delivery cannot be completed, contact us to discuss redelivery or collection. Any additional charge must be explained and agreed before that arrangement proceeds.

05

Product support and service

Contact us about product details, setup questions, order follow-up and purchase support. The scope, cost, expected timing and warranty of any separately requested service must be agreed before it starts. Do not share passwords, one-time codes or unnecessary personal data. Back up your device and remove personal accounts before handing it over for an agreed return or service.

AppleSpace is an independent retailer. An Apple manufacturer warranty or an authorized Apple service is not implied by a purchase from us; confirm any applicable coverage for the specific product.
