A few useful answers.

How do I know a product is available?+

The collection is connected to our shop inventory. Only published, available stock appears online. Final availability is confirmed by our team when we respond to your order request.

What should I check before choosing a phone?+

Check storage, condition, battery health and PTA or carrier-lock status. Ask us to confirm the included items and applicable checking warranty. You can also visit our inner office to test your phone comfortably.

Does checkout take payment?+

Checkout sends an order request to our team. It does not take online payment or reserve stock. We confirm availability, payment details and collection or delivery with you.

Can I collect from the shop?+

Yes. Select collection at checkout, then confirm your visit with us on WhatsApp. Our shop is on the mezzanine floor of Noor Mobile Mall, 6th Road, Rawalpindi.

What are the delivery charges and timings?+

Delivery charges, service areas and timing depend on your location. Our team will confirm them before you finalize your order.

What warranty and returns apply?+

Confirm the checking warranty, exclusions and returns terms for your specific unit with our team before purchasing.

Can I ask for more photos or a video?+

Yes. Message us with the model or listing link. We can help you inspect the unit’s condition and details before your visit.
